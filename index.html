Core Philosophy

A better version of you needs balance: the 5Fs

Just as robust software relies on stable integration layers, a balanced life requires alignment across the core human dimensions. YOU VERSION 2 introduces the 5Fs framework to debug and optimize these key categories:

  • Aligning your spiritual core and belief systems for internal guidance.

  • Optimizing close relationships, building supportive networks, and nurturing bonds.

  • Debugging physical habits, optimizing nutrition, energy levels, and health.

  • Managing resource flows, clearing debt logs, and building security.

  • Strengthening community layers, friendships, and collaborations.
